# STEP 06 Second-use Observer Guide

## Before the child route

Open the parent-only observer at `?observe=hanzi-v2-step06`. The preflight must show the exact STEP 05 feedback identity, final STEP 06 commit, default world route, canonical origin `http://127.0.0.1:5175/`, and the read-only progress-continuity projection.

Choose exactly one separate-session interval:

- `SAME_DAY_SEPARATE_SESSION`
- `ONE_TO_THREE_DAYS`
- `FOUR_TO_SEVEN_DAYS`
- `MORE_THAN_SEVEN_DAYS`

`CONTINUOUS_SAME_SESSION` is invalid. The observer must display: “请在下一次自然、独立的游戏时段使用，不要把它接在第一场后面。” Choose `USE_EXISTING_SETTING`, `START_MUTED`, or `CANCEL`; STEP 06 does not repeat the four-character audio preflight. Review the local privacy notice. `READY` becomes available only after parent authorization, build identity, canonical origin, continuity, interval, sound, and privacy checks succeed. Only the parent READY action may mint the short-lived grant and open the child root.

Codex, agents, and automated browsers may use fixture mode only. They must not click through or conduct a real session.

## Neutral handoff

Use exactly this neutral direction:

> 把游戏交给孩子，先不要指定目的地。
>
> 如果孩子问，可以只说：“你想去哪里都可以。”

Never ask the child to enter Ink Forest, open the spellbook or treasure box, select a card, use a structure slot, or reach a gameplay answer.

## Live dashboard

The adult window may show only current surface (`WORLD`, `SPELLBOOK`, `CLASSIC_HUB`, `FOREST`), current phase, relative time, continuity verified, technically derived first destination, and the persistent `结束本次观察` control. It must not show a preferred destination, correct card, correct structure slot, solver, next-character answer, exact pointer coordinates, raw input, or child identity.

Record human observations with only: `UNRECORDED`, `NOTICED_WITHOUT_PROMPT`, `NOTICED_AFTER_BUILT_IN_SUPPORT`, `NOTICED_AFTER_REGION_ONLY_PROMPT`, `ADULT_ANSWER_REQUIRED`, or `STOPPED`.

- World recognition: recognized world, noticed persistent repairs, selected a destination, understood forest portal, spellbook, treasure box, and return to world.
- Familiarization: remembered core placement, used only built-in hints, needed an adult answer, or showed boredom with a repeated route.
- Engagement: voluntarily continued, explored another world object, or asked for more after the official check.
- Well-being: comfortable, brief confusion recovered, sustained frustration, sensory discomfort, child-initiated stop, and felt forced.

Do not ask a learning quiz. Parent-entered observation never overwrites technically derived first action, first destination, entry, run, or return facts.

## Intervention, stop, and natural end

Allowed intervention codes are `NONE`, `REPEAT_VISIBLE_COPY`, `POINT_TO_REGION_ONLY`, `TECHNICAL_ASSIST`, `ADULT_ANSWER_REQUIRED`, and `STOPPED`. A region-only prompt may identify only `WORLD`, `WORLD_OBJECTS`, `FOREST_PLAYFIELD`, `BOARD`, or `HAND`; never a particular destination, object, card, slot, or answer.

The child sees no official countdown. Show the parent soft guidance: “建议 3–5 分钟以内；不要为了记录而要求孩子完成。” End after one destination-to-world loop, one forest-run-to-world loop, child stop, technical stop, or a natural parent judgment. Stop immediately for distress, sensory discomfort, privacy/continuity failure, or an adult answer requirement.

If the child wants to continue, record `askedForMoreAfterOfficialCheck`, end the formal observation first, and then allow ordinary free play. Free play after that boundary is not part of the official evidence.
