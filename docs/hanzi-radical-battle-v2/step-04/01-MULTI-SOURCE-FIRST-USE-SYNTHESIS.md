# Multi-source first-use synthesis

## STEP 04 child-evidence question

在不改变已接受的玩法、内容和视觉下，能否精确修复拼音 TTS，并建立一套低干扰、本地、可停止、可复现的首次儿童使用观察系统，使真实行为证据可被家长和 ChatGPT解释？

## Four-question gate

1. More like play than practice: yes; the child route removes adult/debug chrome and keeps the accepted world.
2. Character-building remains the core action: yes; observer and audio are consumers, never simulation rules.
3. Understandable with little parent explanation: validation is the real first-use observation; built-in support precedes region-only prompting.
4. Avoids anxiety, manipulation, shame, and compulsive retention: yes; immediate stop, no score/rank/reward countdown, optional questions, and at most one formal replay.

## Explicit exclusions

No real child session, synthetic child conclusion, gameplay/content/visual expansion, new art, default-hub entry, backend, account, upload, recording, profiling, or promotion is part of technical STEP 04 completion.
