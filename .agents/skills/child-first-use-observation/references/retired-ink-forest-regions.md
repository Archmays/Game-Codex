# Retired 墨迹森林 observation regions (historical example)

墨迹森林 is retired (see root `AGENTS.md`). Its second-use contract allowed region-only pointing to `WORLD`, `WORLD_OBJECTS`, `FOREST_PLAYFIELD`, `BOARD` and `HAND`, and its technical facts included chosen ability and camp/spellbook activity.

Use this only as an example of a coarse region list. A new observation package declares its own regions in the active game's observation contract.
