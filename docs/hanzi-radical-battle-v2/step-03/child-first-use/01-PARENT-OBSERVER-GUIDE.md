# Parent observer guide

## Before the child arrives

- Confirm the guarded launcher says the exact review identity is authorized.
- Use a familiar device at comfortable brightness and volume; choose mute or reduced motion if the child normally prefers them.
- Close unrelated tabs and notifications. Do not enter a name, age, school, birthday, photo, audio, or video anywhere.
- Say only: “这里有一段小冒险，你可以自己看看。想停随时可以停。”

## During the run

Sit to the side. Let the child choose click, drag, keyboard, or touch. Avoid pointing at a component or naming the correct slot. If asked what to do, first say “你可以看看画面里哪些地方会回应你。” Only after the built-in layered support appears may you repeat the on-screen line verbatim.

Record observable behavior, not a judgment about intelligence or ability:

- whether the first screen invites an action;
- whether the board and five cards are visually distinct;
- whether `日 + 月 = 明` feels like the action that makes light;
- whether the child notices the top/bottom change for 花;
- whether the chosen ability is noticed in the boss;
- whether the boss intent is understandable without sound;
- whether “try again” remains emotionally safe;
- whether camp repairs and four spellbook pages are noticed.

Do not count errors as a score. The local game event is a design diagnostic, not a grade.

## Intervention codes

- `NONE`: no adult action.
- `REPEAT_VISIBLE_COPY`: repeated an exact on-screen sentence.
- `POINT_TO_REGION_ONLY`: indicated world / board / hand, not a card or slot.
- `STOPPED`: ended immediately under a stop condition.

Free text must avoid personal identifiers. Prefer concrete observations such as “looked between world and board three times” rather than labels such as “confused child.”

## After

Ask only: “你最想再看哪一段？” and “有没有哪一处让你不舒服或不知道发生了什么？” The child may decline. Export the JSON, verify it contains no personal data, and use the return contract. Do not claim acceptance from a single run.
