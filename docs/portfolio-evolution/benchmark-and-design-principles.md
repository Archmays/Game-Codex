# Portfolio Evolution：外部标杆与设计原则

> 访问日期：2026-08-27。产品页面只支持“产品当前宣称/展示了什么机制”，不是独立的儿童效果证据；研究结论也不自动外推到 Game-Codex。

本轮审看 13 个产品案例与 8 个同行评议研究/综述。证据用途是形成审计准则，不是证明儿童喜欢、理解、学会或长期使用。

## 研究证据 matrix

| 来源 | 证据等级 | 支持的结论 | 对现有组合的含义 | 边界 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Habgood & Ainsworth (2011), Motivating Children to Learn Effectively: Exploring the Value of Intrinsic Integration in Educational Games](https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2010.508029) | `R1 PEER REVIEWED EMPIRICAL` | In two studies, an intrinsically integrated game produced better fixed-time learning and substantially more free-choice play than an extrinsic version. | The curriculum operation should be the core game mechanic; this directly motivates the Equation visible-change repair. | Small, specific studies with ages 7-11; not a portfolio-wide causal guarantee. |
| [Plass, Homer & Kinzer (2015), Foundations of Game-Based Learning](https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2015.1122533) | `R2 PEER REVIEWED FRAMEWORK` | Game-based learning depends on interacting cognitive, motivational, affective and sociocultural processes and is distinct from adding gamification. | Audit mechanics, feedback, affect and choice separately; playful styling is not learning evidence. | An integrative framework, not a single causal intervention. |
| [Wouters et al. (2013), A Meta-Analysis of the Cognitive and Motivational Effects of Serious Games](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031311) | `R1 PEER REVIEWED META ANALYSIS` | Across 77 comparisons, serious games had small learning and retention advantages but no significant motivation advantage; supplementation and repeated sessions mattered. | Expect modest conditional effects and use replay/contextual support rather than assuming game format motivates or teaches by itself. | Heterogeneous older studies; not specific to this family portfolio. |
| [Mayer (2019), Computer Games in Education](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010418-102744) | `R2 PEER REVIEWED REVIEW` | Modality, personalization, pretraining, coaching and self-explanation can add value while irrelevant decoration consumes limited processing. | Prefer micro-demonstration, contextual coaching and concise reflection; reduce decoration during difficult actions. | Findings vary by task and setting. |
| [Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski (2006), The Motivational Pull of Video Games](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11031-006-9051-8) | `R1 PEER REVIEWED EMPIRICAL` | Perceived autonomy and competence related to enjoyment and preference; relatedness also mattered in multiplayer contexts. | Offer bounded meaningful choice, interpretable competence feedback and optional family co-play without prize pressure. | Mostly general-game and non-child evidence; not a learning-effect proof. |
| [Shute (2008), Focus on Formative Feedback](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654307313795) | `R2 PEER REVIEWED REVIEW` | Effective formative feedback is supportive, specific, credible and timely, with manageable elaboration focused on learning. | Explain what changed, why it did not work and one calm next action without revealing the full solution. | Broad educational review; timing still needs mechanic-specific validation. |
| [Wood, Bruner & Ross (1976), The Role of Tutoring in Problem Solving](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7610.1976.tb00381.x) | `R1 PEER REVIEWED EMPIRICAL` | Scaffolding can control difficult elements while preserving the child's meaningful work. | Use a fading hint ladder and return control for the final meaningful action. | Small classic tutor-child task; design model rather than automatic digital effect. |
| [Skene et al. (2022), Can Guidance During Play Enhance Children's Learning and Development in Educational Contexts?](https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.13730) | `R1 PEER REVIEWED SYSTEMATIC REVIEW META ANALYSIS` | Guided play exceeded direct instruction for some early-math, shape and task-switching outcomes, but not many others; implementation was heterogeneous. | Combine clear goals with child choice and flexible prompts, especially in Math World. | Results differed by outcome and comparator; no universal child-outcome claim. |

## 进入 Portfolio 审计的设计原则

1. **Learning action must be game action.** Inspect Equation, Make Target, Clock, Array, Pinyin and Memory for detached answer/reward wrappers.
2. **Every legal action needs a perceptible causal consequence.** State identity, representation, feedback and world response must agree.
3. **Use bounded agency.** Offer a recommended next action plus real alternatives, replay and retreat without loss.
4. **Feedback teaches; rewards decorate.** Use immediate semantic feedback and one calm recovery action, not rarity or currency pressure.
5. **Scaffold and fade.** Micro-demo, critical-feature cue, partial hint and optional worked support must preserve the final meaningful action.
6. **A world product needs child-authored consequences.** My Game World remains a hub until it has a learning/play loop; do not invent V2 progression in this cycle.
7. **Keep replay pressure-free.** No daily quests, streaks, FOMO, battle passes, loot rarity or paid advantage.
8. **Separate products, modules, compatibility and engines.** A retained GameDefinition or save key does not imply a child-facing card.
9. **Stay offline, anonymous and sensor-minimal.** Do not import cameras, accounts, cloud profiles, telemetry or proprietary hardware assumptions.
10. **Maintain the evidence boundary.** Machine review cannot establish child enjoyment, comprehension, retention or learning effect.

## 尚不确定

- 商业产品未在本轮独立安装或进行真实儿童首用测试；功能、定价与目录规模可能变化。
- 开放探索与明确进阶的比例必须按 Hanzi、Math、English 各自机制判断，研究不支持一个通用比例。
- My Game World 是否应获得本机持久创作工具，仍是未来产品方向，不由 Toca/Sago/Pok Pok 对照自动决定。
- 相机、麦克风、账户、云档案、遥测与专有硬件继续排除，除非未来另行明确授权并完成隐私/无障碍审计。
