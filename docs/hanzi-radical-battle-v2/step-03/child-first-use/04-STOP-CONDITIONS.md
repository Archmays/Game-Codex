# Stop conditions

Stop immediately, without asking the child to finish, if any of these occur:

- the child says or signals that they want to stop;
- distress, fear, shame, repeated frustration, or physical discomfort is visible;
- flashing, motion, brightness, or sound causes discomfort;
- the browser exposes parent review/debug UI, personal files, a network prompt, account request, advertisement, payment, or unexpected permission request;
- the board, controls, or intent become unusable after resize/orientation change;
- progress cannot be safely resumed and the child would be forced to repeat a cleared phase;
- a caregiver must reveal a component or slot answer to continue;
- any recording, camera, microphone, or upload begins unexpectedly;
- the reviewed identity does not match the launched build.

Use the neutral line: “我们先停在这里，营地会等你。” Record only the stop-category code and an impersonal description. Never frame stopping as failure.
