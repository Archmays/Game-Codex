# Research and Decision

Research refreshed 2026-08-28. Vendor pages describe their own products and are treated as mechanism evidence, not independent proof of outcomes.

## Eight cases

| Case | Useful mechanism | Counter-signal / boundary | Decision here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Toca Boca World](https://www.tocaboca.com/toca-boca-world) | One world map contains many locations and tools for self-directed stories. | Friday gifts and limited-time packs are commercial cadence, not a learning need. | Keep three legible worlds and nested stations; do not copy cadence or collection pressure. |
| [Sago Mini World](https://sagomini.com/world/) | Many activities live in one child-friendly app; downloaded play can be offline; no rules, time limits, or points. | Vendor claims about developmental outcomes are not validation. | Preserve free station order, offline runtime, and calm return/re-entry. |
| [Pok Pok FAQ](https://playpokpok.com/faqs/) | Open-ended digital playroom, gentle sound/motion, no level pressure. | Subscription and vendor safety/addiction claims are outside this repository's evidence. | Prefer intrinsic actions and reduced-motion support; avoid streaks and “one more” pressure. |
| [PBS KIDS Games](https://pbskids.org/apps/play-pbs-kids-games) | A clear catalog groups many games under one trusted destination. | A broad catalog alone does not produce a coherent world or direct learning loop. | Keep Classic as a backup catalog, subordinate to three world destinations. |
| [Khan Academy Kids](https://learn.khanacademy.org/khan-academy-kids/) | A personalized path coexists with an independently explorable library and a separate adult layer. | Adaptive claims and scale do not transfer to a local family game. | Keep child play surfaces free of adult metadata; allow ordered and free-choice navigation to coexist. |
| [DragonBox Numbers](https://dragonbox.com/products/numbers) | Numbers are directly manipulated as embodied objects; the mathematical relation is the play action. | Vendor learning claims are not accepted as causal evidence here. | Protect direct equation, clock, array, and target manipulation instead of adding detached rewards. |
| [Prodigy Math](https://www.prodigygame.com/main-en/prodigy-math) | World, quests, battles, and adaptive practice make a strong navigation shell. | Math questions power battles/quests and daily/seasonal rewards; learning and reward loops can separate. | Use the shell lesson, reject daily/seasonal quests, exclusive rewards, membership pressure, and detached question gates. |
| [ABCmouse Classic](https://support.abcmouse.com/hc/en-us/articles/4919113704855-How-to-Learn-with-ABCmouse-Classic) | A step-by-step path, free exploration, and guided learning coexist. | Tickets, rooms, avatars, pets, and lesson rewards can become an extrinsic economy. | Preserve multiple re-entry paths; do not add currency, shops, pets, or completion prizes. |

## Research principle

The strongest applicable research principle remains intrinsic integration: the curriculum operation should be the action that advances play, not a detachable toll. See [Habgood & Ainsworth (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2010.508029), with the boundary that its specific studies do not prove portfolio-wide outcomes. Feedback should expose actionable state without claiming mastery; see [Shute (2008)](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654307313795). Game design should coordinate mechanics, learning, and affect without assuming automated checks establish child experience; see [Plass, Homer, and Kinzer (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2015.1122533).

## Independent machine reviews

Review A traversed the child-facing journey in a fresh browser and found duplicate Equation identity, wrong return/re-entry, Math focus loss, the fractured Array workbench, and weak visited-state communication.

Review B independently audited portfolio/module/surface/save/route/profile declarations and found false Math station ownership, a Classic checker loophole, ignored `lastLevelId`, current-count drift, and no current lifecycle record.

Agreements: remove only the duplicate card; preserve definitions/routes/saves/content; make runtime/host truth explicit; repair focus and Array layout; avoid Hanzi/English scope without a defect. Difference resolved: Equation is not demoted in quality—it becomes a **flagship module** with the unchanged S profile.

## Six Hats decision

- **White:** baseline gates passed; the defects were hierarchy, metadata, navigation, focus, and layout—not missing game content.
- **Red:** two homes, a generic “大厅” return, scattered Array controls, and lost focus likely weaken continuity; this is an interface inference, not child observation.
- **Black:** deleting the definition, rewriting old evidence, or auto-focusing every direct load would create regressions.
- **Yellow:** five causal Math activities, stable lazy loading, local saves, and existing calm feedback are retained strengths.
- **Green:** explicit runtime/host/save fields, an auditable empty Classic-exception registry, saved-level restore, station-focus keys, and truthful `上次来过 / 继续这里` affordances.
- **Blue:** implement all P0/P1 clusters and seven bounded P2 clusters in at most three rounds; retain the human-evidence boundary.
